## 🎬 FILMING TIPS

### **Camera Setup:**
- **Lighting:** Face a window or use a ring light - no shadows on your face
- **Background:** Clean wall or office, not messy room
- **Camera angle:** Eye level, not looking up or down
- **Distance:** Arm's length away, frame from chest up
- **Recording:** 1080p minimum, 60fps if possible

### **Screen Recording:**
- Use OBS Studio (free) or Loom
- Record at 1920x1080
- Show mouse cursor (important for clicking demonstrations)
- No background noise
- Smooth transitions between camera and screen

### **Delivery Tips:**
1. **Pace:** Speak slightly slower than normal - you're excited, but they need to absorb
2. **Pauses:** After key numbers (€700/month, 3,000 companies, 40x ROI) - pause 2 seconds
3. **Eye contact:** Look at the lens, not the screen
4. **Energy:** Start high energy (hook), drop to serious for problems, build back up for solution
5. **Honesty:** When saying "only 15 companies" or "this is missing" - show genuine concern, not defensive
6. **Confidence:** When showing ACTIA profile and ROI - speak with authority, you know this works

### **Technical Notes:**
- **Before recording:** Clear browser cache, close unnecessary tabs
- **During demo:** Have ACTIA profile open in one tab, another company in another tab for comparison
- **Practice clicks:** Know exactly where you'll click, no fumbling
- **Backup plan:** If live demo fails, have screenshots ready

### **Editing (if needed):**
- Cut long pauses
- Add text overlays for key numbers: "€700/month", "3,000 companies", "40x ROI"
- Add arrows/circles when pointing at signals or scores
- Background music: Subtle, professional (or none - silence is fine)
- Transitions: Simple fades, no flashy effects
