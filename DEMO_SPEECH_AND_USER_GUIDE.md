## 🎯 OPENING: THE PROBLEM WE'RE SOLVING

**Current Situation - What the Sales Manager Does Today:**

*"Every morning, the ABBK sales manager opens Google and searches 'SolidWorks Tunisia' on the SolidWorks website. He sees a list of company names - maybe 50, maybe 100. He writes them down. Then he starts calling.*

*This is not a sales strategy. This is cold calling with a blindfold on."*

**The Cost of This Approach:**
- ⏰ **8+ hours per week** manually researching companies
- 📉 **5-10% conversion rate** because leads are not qualified
- 💸 **Lost deals** because competitors reach the companies first
- 😤 **Sales team frustration** from constant rejection
- 🚫 **No tracking** - no way to know which signals work, which companies to prioritize

**What We Need Instead:**
A system that tells us:
- ✅ **WHO** to call (qualified companies with real buying signals)
- ✅ **WHAT** to offer them (SOLIDWORKS Standard vs Simulation vs Training)
- ✅ **WHY** they'll say yes (they're hiring, expanding, exporting, under audit pressure)
- ✅ **WHEN** to call (new machine purchase, new hire, funding received = NOW)

## 💡 THE SOLUTION: ABBK LeadEngine

**What It Does in One Sentence:**
*LeadEngine is an AI-powered sales intelligence platform that automatically finds Tunisian companies that need SOLIDWORKS, ranks them by conversion probability, and tells you exactly what to offer them.*

### **How It Works - The 4-Step Automated Process:**

**STEP 1: DISCOVERY** - Find all potential customers
- Scrapes Tunisian business directories, job boards, news sites
- Extracts company names, websites, sectors, locations
- Validates data (removes duplicates, fake listings)
- **Current Status:** ✅ Working - 15 real companies in database

**STEP 2: ENRICHMENT** - Gather deep intelligence on each company
- Visits company websites and scrapes all pages (about, team, products, careers)
- Detects: engineering teams, CAD software usage, ISO certification, export activity
- Identifies: hiring activity, expansion news, international clients
- **Current Status:** ✅ Working - 14/15 companies enriched (93% success rate)

**STEP 3: SCORING** - Rank every company for every ABBK product
- Calculates 21 separate scores per company (one per ABBK product/training)
- Different weights per product (SOLIDWORKS Electrical prioritizes automotive wiring companies)
- Scores 0-100 based on business manager priorities:
  - Training potential: 40 points (HIGHEST - primary revenue)
  - New machine purchase: 30 points
  - Hiring engineers: 20 points
  - Multinational + Audit: 15 points each
- **Current Status:** ✅ Working - 315 scores calculated

**STEP 4: RECOMMENDATION** - Tell the sales team exactly what to do
- Generates recommendation text: "Lead with SOLIDWORKS Simulation + Corporate Training"
- Explains WHY: "Company exports automotive electronics to Europe, engineers need simulation tools, international clients require licensed software"
- Provides clickable source URLs proving every signal
- **Current Status:** ✅ Working - All recommendations generated
